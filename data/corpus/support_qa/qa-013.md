# Support Q&A: wash cloudy

**Customer:** How do I wash a double cloudy blanket? It doesn't fit my machine.

**Shop:** Wash it in a tub with cold water and mild liquid detergent, or send it to a dry cleaner. Dry it flat or over two lines in the shade. Never tumble dry it hot, because heat causes pilling.
