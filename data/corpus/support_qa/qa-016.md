# Support Q&A: cancel after dispatch

**Customer:** order cancel karna hai, dispatch ho gaya kya?

**Shop:** Dispatch se pehle cancel free hai. Agar parcel courier ke paas chala gaya hai to cancel nahi ho sakta. Aap receive karke return policy use kar sakte hain.
