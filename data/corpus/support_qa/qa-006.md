# Support Q&A: return opened sheet

**Customer:** I opened the bedsheet packet but didn't use it, colour is not what I wanted. Can I return?

**Shop:** Yes, within 7 days of delivery, as long as it is unused, unwashed, and all pieces are in the original packing. Since it is a change of mind, you pay the return shipping, and the original delivery charge is not refunded.
