# Cotton (سوتی / suti / cotton)

## What it is
A natural fabric woven from cotton thread. Our cotton bed sheets are mostly from Faisalabad mills.

## Feels like
Cool, breathable and soft, and it gets softer with washing. It creases more than synthetic fabric.

## Best for
**Summer**, and for anyone who sleeps hot or has sensitive skin. Cotton is the most comfortable sheet from April to October.

## Thread count
A higher thread count feels smoother. 144 is a normal everyday sheet. 200 and above feels noticeably smoother. The thread count is on the product page when we know it.

## Downsides
It shrinks 2–3% on the first wash, creases, and printed colours can fade over many washes.

## Cotton or poly-cotton?
Poly-cotton is cotton blended with polyester. It is cheaper, creases less and does not shrink, but it is less breathable in summer.
