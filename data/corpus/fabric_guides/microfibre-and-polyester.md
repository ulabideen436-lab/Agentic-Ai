# Microfibre and polyester (مائیکرو / micro / polyester)

## What it is
Very fine synthetic fibres woven into a smooth sheet. Many of our printed 3-piece and 4-piece bed sheet sets are microfibre.

## Feels like
Smooth, slightly silky, and warmer than cotton.

## Best for
All year in moderate weather, and for customers who want bright prints that do not fade, no ironing, and no shrinking.

## Downsides
Less breathable than cotton, so it can feel warm in peak summer.

## "Silk" in product names
Names such as "Italian silk", "American silk" and "Spanish silk" describe a silky finish on polyester. They are **not pure silk**.

## Customers ask
- "ye silk hai asli?" → no, a silky polyester finish.
- "rang to nahi utre ga" → microfibre prints hold their colour well.
