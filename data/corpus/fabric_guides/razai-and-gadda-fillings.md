# Razai and gadda fillings (bharai / بھرائی)

## Cotton filling (روئی / rui / suti)
Traditional cotton fibre, much of it from **Kasur**. It is the warmest and heaviest filling, lasts for years, and can be re-fluffed (dhunai). A cotton razai is sold by the weight of its filling, for example **4 kg**.

## Polyester fibre filling (fibre / فائبر)
Lighter, cheaper and easier to wash than cotton. Less warm for the same thickness. It does not need dhunai, but it flattens slowly over the years.

## How much filling
- Single razai: **3–4 kg** of cotton for a normal winter, **5 kg** for very cold areas.
- Double razai: **5–6 kg** of cotton.
- Gadda: **6–10 kg**, depending on size and thickness.

## Choosing
- Coldest weather and a long life: cotton.
- Light, washable, or for someone who finds cotton too heavy: fibre.

## Customers ask
- "razai me rui hai ya fibre" · "kitne kg ki razai hai" · "Kasur ki rui hai?"
- "which quilt is warmest?" → a cotton-filled razai with 4–5 kg of filling.
