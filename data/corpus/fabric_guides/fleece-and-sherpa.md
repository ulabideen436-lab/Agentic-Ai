# Fleece and sherpa (فلیس / flees / sherpa)

## Fleece
A light, warm polyester fabric with a brushed surface. Irani fleece blankets are a thin, warm, cheaper option. They are good as a second layer or for travel.

## Sherpa
Fleece on one side and a curly, wool-like pile on the other. It is warmer than plain fleece and heavier.

## Best for
Fleece: mild winter, travel, a sofa throw. Sherpa: cold nights on a single bed.

## Compared with cloudy
Both are lighter and thinner than a cloudy blanket, and both cost less.

## Customers ask
- "irani flees kaisa hai" → thin, warm and light.
- "sherpa kya hota hai" → fleece with a wool-like curly side.
