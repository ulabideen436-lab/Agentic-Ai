# Cloudy and mink blankets (کلاؤڈی کمبل / cloudy kambal)

## What "cloudy" means
Cloudy is the market name for a **thick, very soft, fluffy polyester blanket**. The name comes from the cloud-soft feel. Most of our winter blankets are cloudy.

## What "mink" and "plush" mean
Mink and plush blankets are also polyester, with a short, smooth pile that feels like fur. They are lighter than cloudy, and are common in baby blankets.

## Warmth
From warmest to lightest: **2-ply cloudy** > **1-ply cloudy** > **mink / plush** > **fleece**.

## Best for
Cloudy: **December and January**, and cold areas. Mink: early winter, or homes with heating.

## "Box" and "poly bag" packing
A blanket packed in a **box** is usually a gift-quality set, often with matching pillow covers. A **poly bag** blanket is the same kind of blanket in plain plastic packing, and usually costs less.

## Downsides
Synthetic, so less breathable than wool. Can pill (form small balls) if washed hot or tumble dried.

## Customers ask
- "cloudy kambal kya hota hai" → a thick, fluffy, soft polyester blanket.
- "sab se garam kambal konsa hai" → 2-ply cloudy.
- "box wala aur bag wala me kya farq" → the packing, and box sets often include pillow covers.
