# Jersey (جرسی / jersey / jersi)

## What it is
A knitted fabric, like a T-shirt, that **stretches**. It is soft and smooth.

## Where we use it
- **Mattress covers.** The stretch lets one cover fit mattresses up to 10 inches deep.
- **Jersey razai.** A light razai with a jersey shell, for early winter.

## Best for
Anyone who wants a snug, wrinkle-free fit on the mattress.

## Downsides
Less crisp than cotton. It can lose some stretch if dried in hot sun for long periods.

## Customers ask
- "jersi razai halki hai?" → yes, it is a light razai.
- "jersey mattress cover stretch hota hai?" → yes, up to 10 inches deep.
