# Velvet (مخمل / makhmal / velvet)

## What it is
A fabric with a short, dense raised pile that feels soft and looks rich. We sell velvet mainly as **razai covers** and winter bed sheets.

## Types we stock
- **Print velvet:** a printed design on plain velvet. Lightest and cheapest.
- **4 pile velvet:** a thicker, denser pile. Warmer and heavier.
- **Karai velvet:** velvet with embroidery (karai).
- **Sitari or tila velvet:** velvet with sequins (sitara) or metallic thread (tila) work. Usually for wedding and jahez sets.
- **Malai velvet:** a very smooth, creamy-soft finish.
- **Jali velvet:** velvet with a cut-work (jali) pattern.

## Best for
**Winter.** Velvet holds warmth and is the most common razai cover fabric in Pakistan.

## Downsides
Heavier to wash and slow to dry. Embroidered and sequinned velvet must be dry cleaned or hand washed.

## Customers ask
- "makhmal ka razai cover" · "4 pile kya hota hai" → thicker, denser velvet.
- "velvet garmi me use kar sakte hain?" → no, it is too warm; use cotton.
