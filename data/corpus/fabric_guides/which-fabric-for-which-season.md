# Which fabric for which season

## Summer (April to September)
- Sheets: **cotton**.
- Covering: a thin cotton sheet (chadar), or nothing.

## Early winter and spring (October–November, February–March)
- Sheets: cotton or microfibre.
- Covering: **fleece**, **mink** or a **jersey razai**.

## Peak winter (December–January)
- Sheets: microfibre or **velvet**.
- Covering: **cloudy blanket (2-ply)** or a **cotton-filled razai** in a velvet cover.

## Very cold areas (north, hills, Quetta)
- A cotton razai with 5 kg of filling, plus a cloudy blanket on top.

## Customers ask
- "sardi ke liye kya lun" → a cloudy 2-ply blanket or a cotton razai.
- "garmi me konsi chadar" → cotton.
- "what's the warmest setup for Murree?" → a 5 kg cotton razai plus a cloudy blanket.
