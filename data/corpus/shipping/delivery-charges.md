# Delivery charges

Charges are per parcel and depend on the zone and weight. See the delivery zones document for which zone your city is in.

## Zone A: same city
**Rs 150** per parcel, any weight.

## Zone B and Zone C: other cities
**Rs 250** for a parcel up to 3 kg, plus **Rs 100 for each extra kg**.

## Zone D: remote areas
**Rs 400** for a parcel up to 3 kg, plus **Rs 150 for each extra kg**.

## Free delivery
Orders of **Rs 10,000 or more** get free delivery in Zones A, B and C. Zone D orders always pay the delivery charge.

## Heavy items
Razai, gadda and razai sets are heavy. For typical weights, see the parcel weights document. A single-bed razai usually costs Rs 350–450 to deliver outside the city.

## Exception: wholesale orders
Wholesale orders ship by cargo, and the charge is quoted with the order.
