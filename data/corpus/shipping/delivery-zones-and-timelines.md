# Delivery zones and timelines

Timelines are in **working days** after dispatch. Sundays and public holidays do not count.

## Zone A: same city (<STORE_CITY>)
**1–2 working days.** Orders confirmed before 2:00 pm are often delivered the next day.

## Zone B: major cities
Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Peshawar, Hyderabad, Quetta.
**2–4 working days.**

## Zone C: other cities and towns
Any city or town not in Zone B that the courier serves.
**3–6 working days.**

## Zone D: remote areas
Gilgit-Baltistan, remote parts of Azad Kashmir, interior Balochistan and the former tribal districts.
**5–10 working days.** COD may not be available here. We confirm this when you order.

## Before dispatch
Orders are usually dispatched within **1–2 working days** of confirmation. During the winter rush (November to January) this can be 3 working days.
