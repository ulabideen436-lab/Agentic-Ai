# What if my delivery is late or lost

## Late
If your parcel has not arrived **2 working days after** the end of your zone's delivery window, message us with your order number. We raise it with the courier the same day and send you an update within 1 working day.

## Refusing a late COD parcel
If a COD parcel arrives after that point, refusing it counts as a **valid reason**. It does not affect your future orders.

## Lost
If the courier confirms the parcel is lost, or it has not arrived **7 working days after** the end of your zone's window, we send a replacement at our cost. If the item is out of stock, we give a full refund of anything you paid.

## Exception: wrong address or phone switched off
If the courier could not deliver because the address was incomplete or the phone was off, the parcel comes back to us. Re-sending it costs the normal delivery charge.
