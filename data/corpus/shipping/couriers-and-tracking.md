# Couriers and tracking

## Which courier
We use **TCS** and **Leopards** for parcels, and cargo for wholesale and very heavy orders. The courier is chosen by your city. You cannot pick one.

## Tracking number
When your order is dispatched, we send the tracking number on WhatsApp or SMS. You can track it on the courier's website or app.

## Rider contact
The rider calls the phone number on the order before delivering. Keep that phone on and reachable.

## Changing the address after dispatch
This is not possible once the parcel is with the courier. Tell us before dispatch.
