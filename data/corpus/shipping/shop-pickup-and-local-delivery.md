# Shop pickup and same-city delivery

## Pickup from the shop
Choose pickup when you order and collect the order from the shop in <STORE_CITY> during opening hours. There is no delivery charge. Orders are ready for pickup **the same day** if confirmed before 4:00 pm.

## Holding a pickup order
We hold a pickup order for **3 days**. After that it goes back to stock, and any advance is refunded.

## Same-city delivery of heavy items
Inside <STORE_CITY>, gadda and razai sets are delivered by the shop's own rider rather than by courier, for the normal same-city charge of Rs 150.
