# Typical parcel weights

Delivery outside the city is charged by weight, so these weights decide the charge. They are packed weights, rounded up.

## Light items (usually under 3 kg)
- Bed sheet set, single or double: 1–2 kg
- Jae namaz: 0.5 kg
- Dasterkhawan: 0.5–1 kg
- Mattress cover: 1–1.5 kg
- Razai cover: 1.5–2.5 kg
- Curtain, one piece: 1 kg
- Baby blanket: 1–1.5 kg

## Medium items (3–5 kg)
- Single-bed cloudy blanket: 3–4 kg
- Double-bed cloudy blanket: 4–5 kg
- 4-piece bed cover set: 4–5 kg
- Sofa cover set: 3 kg

## Heavy items (over 5 kg)
- Single-bed razai (cotton filled): 5–6 kg
- Gadda: 6–10 kg, depending on size and filling
- Razai sets of 9 to 14 pieces: 12–20 kg. These usually ship by cargo.
