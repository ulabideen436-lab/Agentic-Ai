# Storing winter bedding in summer

## Before storing
Wash the covers and blankets, and sun the razai and gadda for a full day. Store nothing damp. Damp bedding grows mould and smells.

## Storage bags
We sell zipped storage bags:
- **Single blanket bag**: holds one single blanket.
- **Double blanket bag**: holds one double blanket or one double razai cover.
- **Razai set bag**: holds a folded razai and its cover.

## Keeping insects out
Put neem leaves or naphthalene balls in the bag, wrapped in a cloth so they do not touch the fabric.

## In October
Air everything in the sun for a few hours before first use.
