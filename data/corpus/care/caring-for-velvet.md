# Caring for velvet razai covers

## Plain and print velvet
- Hand wash, or machine wash **cold** on a gentle cycle, inside out.
- Do not wring. Press the water out and dry flat in the shade.
- Iron only on the back, on low heat.

## Embroidered, sequinned (sitari) or tila velvet
- **Dry clean only.** Washing loosens sequins and embroidery.

## Removing the cover
Take the cover off the razai before washing. Never wash a razai inside its cover.

## Customers ask
- "velvet cover dho sakte hain?" → plain velvet yes, cold; embroidered velvet must be dry cleaned.
- "sitare nikal jayen ge?" → yes, if washed. Dry clean it.
