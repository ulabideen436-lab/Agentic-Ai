# Washing bed sheets

## Cotton sheets
- Machine wash in **cold or lukewarm water (up to 40°C)**.
- Wash dark and printed sheets separately the first 2–3 times, because some colour may run.
- Dry in the shade. Strong sun fades prints.
- Iron on medium heat while slightly damp.

## Microfibre and poly-cotton sheets
- Machine wash cold.
- No ironing needed. Take them out of the machine promptly and fold.
- Do not use high heat, which can leave a shine.

## Before first use
Wash new sheets once before use to remove the factory finish and soften them.

## Customers ask
- "chadar machine me dho sakte hain?" → yes, cold or lukewarm.
- "rang nikle ga?" → possibly in the first washes; wash dark sheets separately.
