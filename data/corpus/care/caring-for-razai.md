# Caring for a razai

## Do not wash a cotton-filled razai
Water makes the cotton filling clump and it takes days to dry. Keep it clean with a **razai cover**, and wash the cover instead.

## Airing
Put the razai in the sun for **2–3 hours every 2 weeks** in winter. This kills moisture and smell and makes it puff up again.

## Fibre-filled razai
These can be washed. Wash in a tub with cold water and mild detergent, press the water out, and dry flat in the sun, turning it over.

## Re-fluffing (dhunai)
After 2–3 winters, a cotton razai flattens. A **dhunai** (re-carding) by a local cotton carder restores it.

## Customers ask
- "razai dho sakte hain?" → cotton: no, use a cover; fibre: yes.
- "razai sakht ho gayi" → it needs dhunai.
