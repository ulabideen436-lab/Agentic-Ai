# Caring for a gadda

## Keep it covered
Use a mattress cover or a thick sheet over the gadda. A gadda cannot be washed.

## Sun it regularly
Air it in the sun for **3–4 hours once a month**, turning it over halfway through. This keeps it dry, fresh and free of mites.

## Turn it
Flip the gadda and rotate it end to end **every 2 weeks**, so it flattens evenly.

## Dhunai
When it becomes hard or lumpy, usually after 1–2 years of daily use, have it re-carded (dhunai). The same cotton becomes soft and thick again.

## Customers ask
- "gadda sakht ho gaya" → it needs dhunai.
- "gadde se smell aa rahi" → sun it for 3–4 hours on both sides.

## خلاصہ
گدا نہیں دھلتا، اس پر کور یا چادر رکھیں۔ مہینے میں ایک بار 3–4 گھنٹے دھوپ لگوائیں اور ہر دو ہفتے پلٹیں۔ سخت ہو جائے تو دھنائی کروائیں۔
