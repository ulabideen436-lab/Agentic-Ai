# Caring for curtains and sofa covers

## Curtains
- Take the hooks or rings off before washing.
- Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle. Wash malai and velvet curtains by hand, or dry clean them.
- Hang them back while slightly damp, so the folds fall straight without ironing.
- Wash every 3–4 months. Dust builds up in the folds.

## Stretch sofa covers
- Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle.
- Do not tumble dry. Heat ruins the stretch.
- Put them back on while slightly damp for the tightest fit.

## Customers ask
- "parde kaise dhoyen" → cold and gentle; dry clean velvet.
- "sofa cover dhone ke baad dheela ho gaya" → it was dried with heat; air dry only.
