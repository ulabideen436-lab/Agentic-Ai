# Caring for blankets

## Washing
- Wash **cold**, on a gentle cycle, with mild liquid detergent.
- A double cloudy blanket is too big for most home machines. Wash it in a tub or send it to a dry cleaner.
- No bleach, and no fabric softener on mink, which flattens the pile.

## Drying
- Dry flat or over two lines, in the shade.
- **Never tumble dry hot.** Heat causes pilling and melts the soft fibres.

## Keeping it fluffy
Shake the blanket out after drying. Brushing a cloudy blanket gently with a soft brush brings back the fluff.

## How often
2–3 washes per winter is usually enough. Airing it in the sun for an hour freshens it between washes.

## Customers ask
- "cloudy kambal kaise dhoyen" → cold, gentle, and dried in the shade.
- "kambal ke roye ban gaye" → that is pilling, caused by hot washing or drying.
