# Caring for jae namaz and dasterkhawan

## Jae namaz
- Shake out and air regularly.
- Hand wash in cold water with mild detergent when needed. Do not machine wash thick velvet or foam-backed mats.
- Dry flat in the shade. Do not iron.
- Store folded or rolled, pile side in.

## Dasterkhawan
- Wipe plastic-coated dasterkhawan with a damp cloth after every meal.
- Cloth dasterkhawan: machine wash cold, and treat oil stains with dish soap before washing.
- Stitched-edge (sillai) dasterkhawan: wash gently so the edge stitching holds.

## Customers ask
- "janamaz machine me dhoyen?" → hand wash is better.
- "dastarkhwan se tail ka daagh" → dish soap before washing.
