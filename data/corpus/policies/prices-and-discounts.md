# Prices, discounts and price changes

## Which price applies
You pay the price at the time your order is **confirmed**. If the price changes after that, your order is not affected.

## Price changes
Prices change with the season and with supplier rates, most often at the start of winter. The current price is always the one in the shop's live price list.

## Discounts
Retail prices are fixed. Discounts are offered only during announced sales and on wholesale quantities (see the bulk and wholesale policy).

## Different sizes
Single, double and king size of the same design are separate products with separate prices.
