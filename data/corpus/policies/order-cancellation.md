# Order cancellation

## Before dispatch
You can cancel free of charge any time before the order is dispatched. Message us with your order number. Any advance payment is refunded in full within 3 working days.

## After dispatch
Once the parcel is with the courier, the order cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the parcel at delivery, or receive it and use the returns policy. If a COD parcel is refused without a valid reason, your next order must be paid in advance.

## Cancellation by the shop
We may cancel an order if the item turns out to be out of stock, or if we cannot confirm the order with you by phone. You get a full refund of anything you paid.
