# Payment methods

We accept:
- **Cash on delivery.** See the COD policy for limits.
- **Bank transfer** to the shop's account.
- **Easypaisa** and **JazzCash**.
- **Cash or card at the shop** for walk-in customers.

For prepaid orders, send a screenshot of the payment with your order number. The order is confirmed once the payment arrives.

We never ask for your card PIN, OTP or password. Anyone asking for these is not the shop.
