# What each order status means

These are the statuses in the order table. An agent reading an order uses this page to explain the status to the customer.

## pending_confirmation
The order was received but not yet confirmed by phone. We call or message within 1 working day.

## confirmed
The order is confirmed and being prepared. It is usually packed the same or next day.

## packed
Packed and waiting for the courier, who usually collects within 1 working day.

## dispatched
With the courier. You get a tracking number. Delivery follows the timeline for your zone.

## delivered
The courier has marked it delivered. Report any problem within 48 hours.

## refused
The customer refused the parcel at delivery. It comes back to the shop.

## returned
A return has reached the shop. It is inspected, then refunded or exchanged.

## cancelled
Cancelled before dispatch. Any advance is refunded within 3 working days.
