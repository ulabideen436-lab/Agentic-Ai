# Visiting the shop

Zafar Yaqoob Bedding Store is a walk-in shop in <STORE_CITY>. You can see and feel every fabric before you buy.

## Opening hours
**11:00 am to 9:00 pm**, Saturday to Thursday. On Friday the shop is closed until after Jummah prayer, then open from 3:00 pm to 9:00 pm.

## At the shop
- Shop prices are the same as online prices, before delivery charges.
- Walk-in purchases follow the same returns and exchange policies, counted from the day of purchase.
- You can collect an online order from the shop instead of paying for delivery. Tell us when you order.
