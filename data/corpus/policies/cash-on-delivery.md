# Cash on delivery (COD)

## How it works
You pay the full amount in cash to the courier rider when the parcel arrives. The amount is the order total plus the delivery charge.

## Where COD is available
COD is available in every city our courier delivers to. For remote areas, see the delivery zones document.

## Order limit
COD is available on orders up to **Rs 25,000**. Above that, we ask for a **50% advance** by bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash, and you pay the rest on delivery.

## Partial payment
Apart from that advance, we do not split COD payments. You cannot pay part of the amount to the rider.

## Checking the parcel before paying
Courier companies do not allow a parcel to be opened before payment. If the parcel is damaged or looks tampered with on the outside, you may refuse it. If you find a problem after opening it, follow the damaged or wrong item policy.

## If you refuse a COD parcel
When a correct, undamaged parcel is refused, the shop pays delivery both ways. If a customer refuses a COD parcel without a valid reason, their **next order must be paid in advance**.
