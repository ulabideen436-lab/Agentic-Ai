# Damaged or wrong item received

## Report within 48 hours
Tell us within **48 hours** of delivery. After that, we cannot tell whether the damage happened in transit.

## Evidence we need
- Your order number.
- Clear photos of the item and the problem.
- A photo of the parcel's outer packing and the courier label.
- An unboxing video, if you have one. It is not required, but it makes the claim faster.

## What we do
- **Damaged item:** we send a replacement at our cost, or refund you if it is out of stock.
- **Wrong item, or wrong size sent by us:** we send the correct item and arrange pickup of the wrong one, both at our cost.
- **Missing piece from a set:** we send the missing piece at our cost.

## Exception: colour difference
Colours can look slightly different on a phone screen than in person. A small shade difference is not treated as a wrong item. If the design or print is clearly different from what you ordered, it is treated as a wrong item.
