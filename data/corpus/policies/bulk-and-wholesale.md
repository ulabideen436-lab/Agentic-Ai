# Bulk and wholesale orders

## Minimum quantity
Wholesale rates apply from **10 pieces of the same product**. A mixed order qualifies only for the products that reach 10 pieces each.

## Pricing
Wholesale prices are lower than retail and are quoted per order. Send us the product names and quantities on WhatsApp for a quote.

## Payment
Wholesale orders are paid **50% in advance**, with the rest before dispatch. COD is not available on wholesale orders.

## Common bulk buyers
Hostels, madrassas, hotels, wedding sets and resellers. Jae namaz and dasterkhawan are often bought in bulk for mosques and events.

## Delivery
Bulk orders ship by cargo. The delivery charge is quoted with the order, based on weight.
