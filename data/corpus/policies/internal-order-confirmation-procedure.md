# Staff procedure: confirming a new order

Internal. Not for customers.

1. Call or WhatsApp the customer within 1 working day of the order.
2. Confirm the product, size, quantity, delivery address and payment method.
3. For COD orders above Rs 25,000, collect the 50% advance before confirming.
4. Check the customer is not flagged advance-only.
5. Move the order to `confirmed`. If the customer can't be reached after 2 attempts over 2 days, cancel the order.
6. Never read a customer's order details to anyone except the phone number on the order.
