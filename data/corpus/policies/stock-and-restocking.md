# Stock and restocking

## What "out of stock" means
The item is not in the shop right now. It does not mean the product is discontinued.

## Restock time
Regular items are usually restocked within **1–2 weeks**. Winter items (razai, cloudy blankets, velvet razai covers) are stocked from **October to February**. Once they sell out late in the season, they may not come back until next winter.

## Notification
Tell us on WhatsApp which item you want and we will message you when it is back. We do not hold stock without an advance payment.

## Designs and prints
Many bedsheets and blankets come in designs that change every season. A particular print may not come back, even when the product name does.
