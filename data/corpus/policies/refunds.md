# Refunds

## When a refund is issued
After a returned item reaches us and passes inspection, or when we cancel an order we cannot fulfil.

## Method
- **Prepaid orders:** refunded to the same bank account, Easypaisa or JazzCash account the payment came from.
- **COD orders:** there was no online payment, so we refund to a bank account, Easypaisa or JazzCash number you give us. We do not send cash refunds by courier.

## Timeline
Refunds are sent within **3 working days** after the returned item passes inspection. Your bank or wallet may take 1–2 more days to show it.

## What is refunded
The price of the returned items. The original delivery charge is refunded only if the item was faulty, damaged or wrong.

## Exception: partial returns
A set is refunded only when all of its pieces come back. One piece from a set cannot be refunded on its own.
