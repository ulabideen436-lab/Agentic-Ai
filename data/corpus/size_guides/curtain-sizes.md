# Curtain sizes (پردہ / parda / curtain)

## Ready-made curtain (one piece)
**54 inches wide × 90 inches long (137 × 229 cm).**
One piece covers a window up to about 3 ft wide, with gathers. Use 2 pieces for a 5 ft window.

## Curtain cloth by the metre
Cloth is **60 inches (152 cm) wide** and cut to the length you ask for. The price is per metre.

## How much cloth for a window
Take the window width and double it, to allow for gathers. Take the height from the rod to where you want the curtain to end, and add 8 inches for stitching.

## Exception: cut cloth
Cloth cut to length cannot be returned. See the returns policy.

## Customers ask
- "parda kitna lamba hai" → 90 inches.
- "5 foot khirki ke liye kitne parde" → 2 pieces.
- "kapra meter me milta hai?" → yes, 60 inches wide.
