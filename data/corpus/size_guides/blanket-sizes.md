# Blanket sizes (کمبل / kambal / blanket / cloudy)

"Cloudy" is a kind of thick, soft blanket. Cloudy blankets use the same sizes as other blankets.

## Single bed blanket
**63 × 87 inches (160 × 220 cm).**
For one person on a single bed.

## Double bed blanket
**87 × 94 inches (220 × 240 cm).**
For two people on a double bed. This is the size of most blankets named "double bed" or "double".

## King size blanket
**94 × 102 inches (240 × 260 cm).**
For a king bed, or for a double bed when you want the blanket to hang lower on both sides.

## Ply (layers)
Blankets marked **1 ply** are a single layer. **2 ply** blankets are two layers joined together, and are heavier and warmer. The ply is on the product page when we know it.

## Shrinkage
Polyester and mink blankets do not shrink. They can lose softness if washed hot. See the blanket care guide.

## Customers ask
- "double kambal ka size" → 220 × 240 cm.
- "single bed blanket kitna bara hai" → 160 × 220 cm.
- "cloudy blanket aur normal me size ka farq hai?" → no, the sizes are the same.
