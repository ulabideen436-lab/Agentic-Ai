# Bed sheet sizes (chadar / بیڈ شیٹ)

Our bed sheets are **flat sheets**, which are tucked under the mattress, not fitted with elastic. Measurements are for the sheet itself.

## Single bed sheet
**60 × 90 inches (152 × 229 cm).**
Fits a single bed or a charpai up to 3.5 ft wide (42 in), with a mattress up to 6 inches thick.

## Double bed sheet
**90 × 100 inches (229 × 254 cm).** This is the size customers call "90 by 100".
Fits a double bed 5 to 6 ft wide (60–72 in). On a 6 ft bed, the mattress must be no more than 6 inches thick. This is the standard Pakistani double bed sheet.

## King size bed sheet
**108 × 108 inches (274 × 274 cm).**
Fits a king bed of 6 to 6.5 ft wide (72–78 in). Also use it on a 6 ft double bed whose mattress is thicker than 6 inches.

## Pillow covers in sets
**18 × 28 inches (46 × 71 cm).** Fits a standard pillow.

## Compared with foreign sizes
- A US or UK **queen** mattress (60 × 80 in) takes our **double** sheet.
- A US **king** mattress (76 × 80 in) takes our **king** sheet.
- A US **twin** mattress (38 × 75 in) takes our **single** sheet.

## Shrinkage after washing
Pure cotton sheets can shrink **2–3%** on the first wash. That is about 2–3 inches on a double sheet. Poly-cotton and microfibre sheets do not shrink noticeably.

## Customers ask
- "double bed ka size kya hai" → 90 × 100 inches.
- "90 by 100 kis size ka hai" → double.
- "will this fit a queen bed" → yes, use the double.
- "king size kitna bara hota hai" → 108 × 108 inches.
- "single bed sheet charpai pe aa jaye gi?" → yes, up to 3.5 ft wide.
