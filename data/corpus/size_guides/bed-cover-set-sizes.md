# Bed cover set sizes and contents (بیڈ کور / bed cover)

A bed cover is a thick, quilted top cover laid over the bed sheet. It is not a sheet you sleep on.

## 4-piece bed cover set
Contains **1 quilted bed cover, 1 bed sheet and 2 pillow covers**.
- Bed cover: **90 × 100 inches (229 × 254 cm)**
- Bed sheet: **90 × 100 inches (229 × 254 cm)**
- Pillow covers: **18 × 28 inches (46 × 71 cm)**
Fits a standard double bed.

## 8-piece and 12-piece sets
These add cushion covers, a runner, and in 12-piece sets, extra pillow covers, on top of the 4-piece contents. They are usually bought as **wedding sets** (jahez). The exact pieces are listed on each product page.

## Customers ask
- "4 piece me kya kya hai" → bed cover, bed sheet, 2 pillow covers.
- "bed cover aur bedsheet me kya farq hai" → a bed cover is a thick quilted top layer.
- "jahez ke liye konsa set" → 8-piece or 12-piece.
