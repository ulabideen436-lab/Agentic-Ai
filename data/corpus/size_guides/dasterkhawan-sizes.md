# Dasterkhawan sizes (دسترخوان / dastarkhwan / table spread)

## Standard dasterkhawan
**36 × 54 inches (91 × 137 cm).**
Seats 4 people on the floor.

## Large dasterkhawan
**54 × 90 inches (137 × 229 cm).**
Seats 6–8 people. Used for family meals and dawat.

## Sold by the dozen for bulk
Dasterkhawan are packed **12 to a pack** for bulk buyers such as mosques and caterers. Single pieces are also sold.

## Customers ask
- "dastarkhwan kitne logon ka hai" → 4 people standard, 6–8 people large.
- "darjan me milta hai?" → yes, 12 per pack.
