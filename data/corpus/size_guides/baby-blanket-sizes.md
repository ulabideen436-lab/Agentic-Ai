# Baby blanket sizes (بچوں کا کمبل / bachon ka kambal)

## Small baby blanket
**30 × 40 inches (76 × 102 cm).**
For a newborn, a cot or a pram, up to about 1 year.

## Large baby blanket
**40 × 55 inches (102 × 140 cm).**
For a toddler of 1 to 4 years, or a larger cot.

## Newborn blanket
Blankets named "new born" are the small size, and are thinner and lighter.

## Customers ask
- "bache ke liye konsa size" → small up to 1 year, large from 1 to 4 years.
- "baby blanket large kitna bara hai" → 40 × 55 inches.

## خلاصہ
