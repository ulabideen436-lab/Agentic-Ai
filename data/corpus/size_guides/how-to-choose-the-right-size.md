# How to choose the right size

## Step 1: measure the mattress, not the bed frame
Measure the mattress **width**, **length** and **depth** (thickness) in inches. The frame is often wider than the mattress, and the sheet has to fit the mattress.

## Step 2: add the depth twice, plus tuck-in
A flat sheet has to cover the top of the mattress, go down both sides, and tuck under.
**Sheet width needed = mattress width + (2 × depth) + 6 inches to tuck in.**

Example: a 60-inch-wide, 8-inch-deep mattress needs 60 + 16 + 6 = **82 inches**. Our **double** sheet (90 inches wide) fits.

## Step 3: match the result to a size
- Sheet width needed **up to 60 inches** → single (60 × 90 in).
- **61 to 90 inches** → double (90 × 100 in).
- **91 to 108 inches** → king (108 × 108 in).

## For a razai or blanket
Pick by the number of people, not the mattress. One person → single. Two people → double.

## For a mattress cover
Match the mattress width and depth to the mattress cover size guide.

## Questions to ask the customer
When a customer wants a size recommendation, ask:
1. How wide is the mattress, in inches or feet?
2. How thick is the mattress?
3. Is it for a charpai, a bed or the floor?

## Customers ask
- "mera bed 6 foot ka hai konsi chadar" → measure the mattress. A 72-inch mattress 6 inches deep needs 90 inches, so the double fits. If it is 8 inches deep, it needs 94 inches, so choose king.
- "size kaise pata karun" → measure the mattress width and depth.
