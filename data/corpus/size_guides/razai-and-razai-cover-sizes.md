# Razai and razai cover sizes (رضائی / razai / rajai / quilt)

## Single razai
**60 × 90 inches (152 × 229 cm).**
For one person, on a single bed or charpai.

## Double razai
**90 × 100 inches (229 × 254 cm).**
For two people on a double bed.

## Single razai cover
**62 × 92 inches (157 × 234 cm).**
Fits a single razai. The cover is 2 inches larger each way, so the razai goes in easily and does not bunch up.

## Double razai cover
**92 × 102 inches (234 × 259 cm).**
Fits a double razai.

## Is the cover sold separately?
Yes. Razai covers are separate products. A razai is sold **without** a cover unless the product page says "with cover" or it is a razai set.

## Shrinkage
Cotton-filled razai do not shrink, because they are not machine washed. Velvet and cotton razai covers can shrink **1–2%**. Wash them cold.

## Customers ask
- "razai cover alag milta hai kya" → yes, separately.
- "double razai ka size" → 90 × 100 inches.
- "single rajai ka cover kitna bara hai" → 62 × 92 inches.
- "does the quilt come with a cover?" → no, unless it says "with cover".
