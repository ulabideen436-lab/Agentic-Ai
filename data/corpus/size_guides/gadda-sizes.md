# Gadda sizes (گدا / gadda / gaddha / mattress)

A gadda is a cotton- or fibre-filled floor or charpai mattress. It is not a spring or foam mattress.

## Single gadda
**36 × 72 inches (3 × 6 ft, 91 × 183 cm), 3 inches thick.**
Fits a charpai, a single bed, or floor seating for one person.

## Double gadda
**54 × 78 inches (4.5 × 6.5 ft, 137 × 198 cm), 3–4 inches thick.**
Fits a double bed frame as a soft top layer, or floor bedding for two.

## Thickness settles with use
A new cotton gadda is thicker when it arrives and settles by about 1 inch within the first weeks. Getting it re-fluffed (dhunai) brings the thickness back. See the gadda care guide.

## Customers ask
- "gadda kitna mota hai" → 3–4 inches.
- "charpai pe single gadda aa jaye ga?" → yes.
- "is gadda a foam mattress?" → no, it is cotton or fibre filled.
