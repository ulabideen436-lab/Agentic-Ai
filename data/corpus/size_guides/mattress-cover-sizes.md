# Mattress cover sizes (میٹرس کور / mattress cover / fitted cover)

Mattress covers are **fitted**, with elastic all round, and protect the mattress underneath the bed sheet.

## Single mattress cover
**Fits a mattress of 36–42 × 78 inches (91–107 × 198 cm), up to 8 inches deep.**

## Double mattress cover
**Fits a mattress of 60–72 × 78 inches (152–183 × 198 cm), up to 8 inches deep.**

## Jersey mattress covers stretch
Jersey covers stretch, so they also fit mattresses up to **10 inches deep**.

## How to measure
Measure the mattress width, length and depth, not the bed frame. See the "how to choose the right size" guide.

## Customers ask
- "mattress cover mere 8 inch mattress pe aa jaye ga?" → yes, and up to 10 inches for jersey.
- "fitted hai ya flat?" → fitted, with elastic.
