# Jae namaz sizes (جائے نماز / janamaz / musalla / prayer mat)

## Standard jae namaz
**70 × 110 cm (about 27.5 × 43 inches).**
Fits an adult of average height. It is the most common size.

## Large jae namaz
**80 × 120 cm (about 31.5 × 47 inches).**
For taller adults, or for anyone who wants more room for sajda.

## Children's size
We do not stock a separate children's size. Children use the standard size.

## Customers ask
- "janamaz ka size kya hai" → 70 × 110 cm, or 80 × 120 cm for the large one.
- "bara musalla hai?" → yes, 80 × 120 cm.
- "which prayer mat for a tall person?" → the 80 × 120 cm one.
