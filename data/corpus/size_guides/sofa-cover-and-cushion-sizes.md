# Sofa cover and gol takiya sizes

## 5-piece sofa cover set
Covers a **5-seater sofa: one 3-seater and two 1-seaters**. The covers are stretch fabric and fit seats from **70 to 90 inches wide (3-seater)** and **30 to 45 inches wide (1-seater)**.

## Gol takiya (round bolster pillow)
**7 inches across × 20 inches long (18 × 51 cm).**
Used on a sofa or diwan, and as a side pillow on floor seating.

## Customers ask
- "sofa cover 5 seater hai?" → yes, one 3-seater and two 1-seaters.
- "mera sofa bara hai, aa jaye ga?" → yes, if the 3-seater is up to 90 inches wide.
- "gol takiya ka size" → 7 × 20 inches.
