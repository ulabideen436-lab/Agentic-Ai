A wrong number here gets answered confidently to customers, so confirm each one, edit the document, and set `verified: true`.

## 4. Size guides: measure real stock

Every measurement in `size_guides/` is a common market size, not a measurement of this shop's stock. Measure one of each and correct them. This matters most for:
- Bed sheet single, double and king sizes, and the pillow cover size.
- Razai and razai cover sizes.
- Blanket single, double and king sizes (in cm).
- Gadda sizes and thickness.

The two jae namaz sizes (70 × 110 and 80 × 120 cm) come from real product names and are likely right.

## 6. Product pages (generated)

Generated by `scripts/build_corpus.py` from the POS export. The script only fills what the product name reveals. For each page, fill in what the name can't tell you:
- **Fabric**, where it says "Not yet recorded by the shop".
- **Set contents** for 4-piece bed sheet sets, boxed sets, 8/12-piece bed covers and 9/14-piece razai sets.
- **Size**, where the name doesn't say single, double or king.
- **Urdu spelling** of the product's brand name. Today the Urdu line shows only the product type in Urdu.

Also fix these in the POS catalogue itself:
- Duplicate names with different prices. The script prints them, for example "FOMI BEDSHEET" ×3 and "JERSEY MATTRESS COVER" ×2.
- 77 velvet and other items with no product type. They are left out of the product pages on purpose.
